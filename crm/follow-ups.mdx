CRM tracks two different reasons to contact a driver: a **follow-up plan** your team set for the list, and a **callback you promised** for a specific date. A plan tells you the next call or text to make. It doesn't call or send messages automatically.

## Read the three columns

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Last contact** | The latest relevant call, text, or reply, with its result |
| **Follow-ups** | Completed steps in the current plan, not a lifetime count of calls |
| **Next follow-up** | The reply, promise, or plan step that needs attention next |

A call step completes when you attempt a call on or after it's due. A text step needs a text. Calling early, repeatedly dialing the same day, or calling when a text is due doesn't automatically complete more steps. Failed or disconnected-number attempts don't complete a step.

## Promise a callback

Click **Next follow-up** on the row or in the lead panel. Choose a preset, or **Pick date…** for a day and optional time. Check the displayed date before saving.

Using example data: open **Next follow-up**, choose **Pick date…**, select a day, and click **Set**. Check the saved promise before moving on.

Dates and times use the workspace's calendar and time zone. They aren't automatically converted to each driver's location. If a driver asks for “3 PM my time,” confirm their time zone and choose the corresponding workspace time.

Presets such as **2 days** count working days. A promise appears in Today on its due day. **Overdue** turns red after that day has passed; it isn't a minute-by-minute lateness alarm.

A qualifying call attempt or sent text at or after the promised time can clear the promise. The call doesn't have to be answered. An earlier attempt can leave it standing. Check **Next follow-up** afterward, especially if you sent a text but still owe the driver a call.

## Follow the plan

A list usually carries its own copy of the team's workflow. If an Owner or Admin handed it out with **Workflow starts: Not yet**, it has no steps until they apply the workflow; until then only replies and promised callbacks reach Today. A plan might say call, wait two working days, call again, then text. Your list's steps are the ones to follow, even if a different list has another sequence.

Waits start from the preceding completed step. If you do a step late, later steps move later too; Vocal doesn't silently skip them. A late plan step doesn't turn red like a missed promise.

Some plans use waits in hours instead of working days. An hour-based wait is elapsed time and can reach a weekend or day off. Read the due date and time before contacting the driver.

An Owner or Admin edits the team's **Workflow** and can apply changes to selected existing lists. Don't assume a workflow edit has changed every list you already hold.

## What the labels mean

| Label | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Reply now** | Respond to the driver's incoming contact; just opening the lead doesn't answer it |
| **Promised…** | Keep the callback you set |
| **Call #2** or **Text #1** | Complete that step of the list's plan |
| **No next step** | Set a callback if more work is needed; there may be no plan, or a conversation stopped it |
| **Plan done** | All steps are complete; choose what to do next rather than waiting for an automatic restart |
| **Stopped** | Check the status or reason before trying to resume |

## Why did the plan stop?

A connected conversation, or responding to a waiting reply, stops the plan. It doesn't resume by itself a few days later. You can set a promise or choose **Restart plan** when another sequence is appropriate.

A stopping status, **Stop following up**, a dead number, do-not-call, or text opt-out can also stop work. A team's status names and stopping rules can differ. See [Access and stopping contact](/crm/privacy-and-stopping).

## Skip, restart, or stop

- **Skip this step** moves to the next step and counts its wait from now. It doesn't place a call or send a text.
- **Restart plan** begins again at the first step. It resets progress for this run; earlier activity remains in history.
- **Stop following up** removes the next planned action without deleting the lead or its history.
- **Resume following up**, when available, isn't the same as restarting at step one. Check the next action it shows.

Restarting or changing a date doesn't remove a do-not-call or opt-out restriction.

## Will Vocal remind me?

Use **Today** as your work list. Don't rely on a CRM callback date to send a separate reminder notification. If you need a task with a reminder, use [Inbox → Tasks](/inbox#tasks); a task and a CRM callback are separate records.
