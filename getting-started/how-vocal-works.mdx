Vocal is the phone system your team calls and texts from. Every conversation lands in one shared place with a transcript and a summary, so a manager can see how the week went, or a recruiter can pick up where a teammate left off, without asking around.

## Your workspace and team

A **workspace** is your business on Vocal. It holds your numbers, calls, texts, contacts, and people.

Everyone in it is a **member** with one of three roles:

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Full access, including billing, plan changes, and workspace settings. Every workspace has exactly one. |
| **Admin** | Runs the phone system: numbers, integrations, team, and live calls. No billing or plan access. |
| **Member** | Makes and answers calls, texts, and works contacts and CRM leads. Sees their own stats and personal settings, but not workspace settings. |

A **manager** isn't a role. It's someone who manages a [group](/team/groups): they can see the calls, texts, and stats for the numbers in that group, and nothing beyond it. See [Members and roles](/team/members-and-roles).

## Numbers and the people on them

A **number** is what your customers dial and text. Each member on a paid plan gets one number included, and you can add shared numbers for a desk or a team.

Every number has **Users**, the people on it. They're who the number rings, and they're who can see its calls and texts. Owners and Admins see every number. A recruiter on the recruiting line sees that line's conversations; a dispatcher who isn't on it doesn't.

## The Inbox

Calls, texts, and tasks from your numbers land in the **Inbox**:

- **Chats** holds text conversations, one thread per pair of your number and theirs.
- **Calls** lists calls with the transcript, an AI summary, and the recording on plans that include it.
- **Tasks** collects follow-ups pulled from calls, linked back to the conversation they came from.

See [Your Inbox](/inbox).

## Call flows

A **call flow** decides what happens when someone calls a number. Every new number starts with the same one: ring the number's Users for about 20 seconds, then send the caller to voicemail. Owners and Admins can add business hours or send after-hours calls somewhere else. See [Call flows](/calls/call-flows).

## How AI helps

AI works in the background on your team's conversations:

- Calls get live transcripts and AI summaries.
- [Iris](/iris) answers questions like "Which applicants did we talk to today?" from your real calls and texts, with links to the conversations it used.

## Where things live in the app

| In the sidebar | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Search** | Find a person, call, or conversation |
| **Activity** | Your notifications |
| **Inbox** | Chats, calls, and tasks |
| **CRM** | Recruiting leads and follow-ups, when your workspace uses it |
| **Settings** | Your account, numbers, team, and workspace |
| **Contacts** | People you talk to, personal and shared |
| **Keypad** | Dial a number |
| **Text** | Start a new text |
| **Calls** | Your call history (the same list as **Inbox → Calls**) |
| **Voicemail** | Voicemails left on your numbers |
| **Iris** | Ask anything about your calls, texts, and team |
| **Analytics** | Call and text activity; members see their own |

Owners, Admins, and group managers also see **Alerts** and **Live**, which shows calls happening now.
