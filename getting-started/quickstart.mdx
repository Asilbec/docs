## Overview

Vocal is an AI-native business phone that connects information scattered across conversations into a shared understanding of the business. Your team calls and texts from Vocal, and every conversation lands in one shared place, so nobody has to chase updates. This guide covers what makes Vocal different, how to set it up, and where to go next.

## Why Vocal?

- **Shared by default.** Numbers belong to the team. Everyone on a number sees its calls and texts, so a recruiter or dispatcher can pick up exactly where a teammate left off.
- **Everyone stays informed.** Calls get a transcript and a summary that anyone on the number can read. On the Scale plan, [Iris](/iris) answers questions like "Which applicants did we talk to today?" with links to the calls behind the answer.
- **Built for recruiting.** The [CRM](/crm/getting-started) puts your driver leads, replies, and promised callbacks in one **Today** list, so nobody falls through the cracks.

## How does Vocal work?

Vocal is an internet phone system. Your calls and texts run through the Vocal app in your browser or the [desktop app](/apps/desktop), so all you need is a computer with a microphone. There are no desk phones or special hardware.

Your numbers are real US phone numbers. Customers and drivers call and text them like any other number, and you can add teammates, share numbers, and change who rings at any time.

## Get set up in 4 steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your workspace">
    Go to [vocal.solutions](https://www.vocal.solutions) and click **Start free**. Answer **How will you use Vocal?**, fill in **Your business**, and on **Say hello to your new number** type a US area code and pick a number.

    Then choose **Starter**, **Growth**, or **Scale** and add a card. Your first subscription starts with 14 days free, and the card isn't charged until the trial ends. See [Plans and billing](/billing/plans).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register for texting">
    US carriers only deliver business texts from registered senders. An Owner or Admin opens **Settings → Compliance** and fills in the business details. Approval usually takes less than a week, so start this early. See [SMS registration](/numbers/sms-registration).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Open **Settings → Members** and invite people by email as an **Admin** or **Member**. Each member on a paid plan gets one number included, which you assign in **Settings → Numbers**. See [Members and roles](/team/members-and-roles).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get the app and make a test call">
    Install the [desktop app](/apps/desktop) so calls ring even when your browser is closed. Open **Keypad**, call your own mobile, and talk for a few seconds. Then open **Inbox → Calls**: the call is there with its transcript and summary.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Moving your team off RingCentral? Pick any number during setup. Afterward you can [transfer your existing numbers](/numbers/porting) and keep the ones your drivers and customers already know.
</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need special hardware?">
    No. A computer with a microphone is enough. A headset makes long calling sessions easier.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need internet for calling and texting?">
    Yes. Vocal runs over the internet, so use a steady connection for calls.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Vocal use minutes from my mobile plan?">
    No. Calls and texts go through the Vocal app, not your mobile carrier.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How long is the free trial?">
    14 days on your workspace's first subscription. You add a card up front, and it isn't charged until the trial ends.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I keep my existing phone number?">
    Yes. [Transfer it to Vocal](/numbers/porting) from RingCentral or another carrier.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens when someone calls and nobody answers?">
    A new number rings its people for about 20 seconds, then the caller reaches voicemail. An Owner or Admin can change that with a [call flow](/calls/call-flows).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How much does Vocal cost?">
    Plans are priced per member per month, and texts are billed by usage. See [Plans and billing](/billing/plans).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
