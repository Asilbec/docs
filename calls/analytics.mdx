Open **Analytics** in the sidebar. What you see depends on your role:

| Who | What they see |
|---|---|
| Owners and Admins | **Team analytics**: workspace call volume and each member's activity. |
| Members | **My analytics**: the same page, limited to their own calls and texts. |
| Group managers | Their own **My analytics**, plus a **Scope** filter to switch to a group they manage. |

Owners and Admins also get the **Scope** filter once the workspace has groups. See [Groups](/team/groups) for how managers are set up.

## Team and personal analytics

Pick a **Date** range at the top: **Today**, **Last 7 days** (the default), **Last month**, or **Last 3 months**. The **Inbox** filter limits everything to the numbers you choose. Each figure is compared with the previous period of the same length, and dates follow your workspace's timezone, which is set when the workspace is created. To change it, email [team@vocal.solutions](mailto:team@vocal.solutions). Today's charts go hour by hour, and Last 3 months shows weekly bars.

The cards are grouped into:

- **Call activity**: **Total calls**, **Outgoing calls**, and **Answered calls**.
- **Connection rates**: **Answer rate** and **Connected rate**.
- **Call duration**: **Time on calls** and **Avg call time**.
- **Messaging**: **Texts sent** and **Texts received**.

Below them, **User activity** lists each person with **Total**, **Outgoing**, **Answered**, **Talk time**, and **Texts sent**, plus the change from the previous period. Click **Total** to sort, use the filter to look at one measure (for example **Missed calls**), or click a name for that person's detail. **Busy times** is a heatmap of when your team is most active, which helps when you're deciding how many recruiters to keep on the phones in the late afternoon.

**Export CSV** downloads the user activity for the selected range.

## A group you manage

Choose a group under **Scope** to see only the work on that group's numbers. The **Date** choices here are **Past 24 hours**, **Past 7 days**, and **Past 30 days**. You get totals for **Calls**, **Answered**, **Texts sent**, and **Texts received**, and a **Teammate activity** table. A teammate's work on numbers outside the group isn't counted. The report covers the range up to when you opened it, so click **Refresh** to include newer activity.
