A call flow is a number's plan for incoming calls. Every number gets one when you buy it: **Ring users** rings everyone on the number for 20 seconds, and if nobody picks up, the caller goes to **Voicemail**. You can change that for any number.

Only Owners and Admins can edit call flows.

## Open a number's call flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the number">
    Go to **Settings → Numbers** and click the number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Call flow tab">
    The tab shows a preview of the flow that's live on this number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit it">
    Hover the preview and click **Edit call flow**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A number can hold several flows, but only one is **Live**. Use **New flow** to build an alternative, like a holiday schedule, without touching the live one.

## Steps you can use

Click or drag a step from the **Steps** panel. It attaches to an open branch, so every branch always leads somewhere. Select a step to change its settings on the right.

| Step | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Business hours** | Splits calls into **During hours** and **After hours**. Pick a timezone and set open hours for each day. |
| **Ring users** | Rings teammates' Vocal apps at once, and the first to answer takes the call. Choose **Everyone on this number** or **Specific teammates**, and how long to ring. If nobody answers, the call follows **If call is missed**. |
| **Voicemail** | Plays a greeting and records a message. |
| **Hang up** | Ends the call. |

Some older flows contain a **Ring group** step. It still rings its group, but you can't add new ones. [Ringing your team](/calls/ringing-your-team) covers who rings and how.


### Voicemail step

Select the step and click **Set greeting** to upload a file, record one, or generate one with **Text to speech**. You can also type a greeting in the box and Vocal reads it aloud. A greeting on this step replaces the number's usual greeting, which is handy for an after-hours message that gives your on-call dispatcher's hours.

**Transcribe voicemails** is on by default. See [Voicemail](/calls/voicemail) for where messages land.

## Publish your changes

As soon as you edit, a bar says **This call flow has unpublished changes**. Click **Publish changes** to make them live, or **Discard changes** to go back to the last saved version. Cmd+S (Ctrl+S on Windows) saves a draft without publishing.

If the flow isn't the number's live flow yet, publishing only saves a new version. Switch the number over with **Set live** on the **Call flow** tab, or **Set as live** in the flow menu at the top left of the editor. Changes affect only this number.

## Hold music

Below the flow preview, **Hold music** sets what callers on this number hear while they wait: when a teammate puts them on hold. Choose **Upbeat** or **Mellow**, or click **Upload custom track** to upload a file (up to 5 minutes), record one, or use text to speech. Only upload audio you have the rights to.

## Example: a recruiting line

Start with **Business hours** set to your office hours. Send **During hours** to **Ring users** with **Everyone on this number**, and let **If call is missed** go to **Voicemail**. Send **After hours** straight to **Voicemail** with a greeting that says when the office opens.
