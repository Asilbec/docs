Texts live in **Inbox**, under **Chats**. Each conversation pairs one of your numbers with one outside number, so if a driver applicant texts both your Dallas and Atlanta recruiting lines, you'll see two separate conversations.

<Note>
  To text US numbers, your business needs [SMS registration](/numbers/sms-registration). Until then, incoming texts still arrive but outgoing US texts are blocked. Text from a local number.
</Note>

## Who sees which conversations

A number's conversations belong to the people on that number's **Users** tab. They see its texts and get notified when a new one comes in. Owners and Admins can see every number's conversations, and group managers can see the numbers in groups they manage (see [Groups](/team/groups)).

The **Inbox** filter at the top of Chats starts on **My inboxes**. If you can see more than your own numbers, switch it to **All inboxes**, a group, or a teammate.

## Send a text

Click **New conversation** at the top of Chats, enter a number or pick a contact, and write your message. The composer shows which of your numbers it's sending from; you can only send from numbers you have access to. A few extras sit in the composer:

- **Attach file** sends photos and videos, up to 10 per message. Photos, PDFs, contact cards, voice notes, and videos you receive show up as what they are.
- **Personal snippets** are saved replies only you can see. Type `/` at the start of a word to insert one, or save a draft you keep retyping, like the next steps you send every applicant after a phone screen.
- **AI suggested reply** (the sparkles button) drafts a few replies based on the conversation, including recent calls with that person. Pick one and it drops into your draft for you to edit before sending.

## Keep Chats tidy

Click **More conversation actions** in a conversation's header for these:

| Action | Who it affects |
| --- | --- |
| **Mark as done** | Everyone who can see the conversation. A new incoming text reopens it. |
| **Pin conversation** | Only you |
| **Mark as unread** | Only you |
| **Archive for me** | Only you. Undo with **Restore to my inbox**. |
| **Block number for me** | Only you. Its calls stop ringing you and its text, missed-call, and voicemail alerts go quiet; teammates and the conversation history aren't affected. Your blocked list is in **Settings → Blocked**. |
| **Delete conversation** | Everyone. Owners and Admins only. |

To find an old text, click **Search** in the left rail; it searches your calls, texts, and contacts together.

## Consent and cost

Only text people who gave you their number and agreed to hear from you, and stop when someone replies STOP. [SMS registration](/numbers/sms-registration) covers consent wording and the STOP and HELP replies. Texts are billed per SMS segment; the rate is on [Plans and billing](/billing/plans).
