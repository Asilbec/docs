Vocal connects your phone line to the tools your business runs on. We keep this page honest: **Live** means you can use it today; **Building** means it's actively in development — not vaporware, not "coming soon" forever.

## Live today

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Google Calendar" icon="calendar">
    Your agent checks real availability and books appointments directly on your calendar, during the call.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Webhooks & custom tools" icon="bolt">
    The connect-to-anything escape hatch: your agent can call any HTTP endpoint mid-conversation — send data to your CRM, look up an order, trigger any system that has an API or an automation platform in front of it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Built-in CRM actions" icon="address-book">
    Agents tag contacts, leave notes, and update contact fields automatically as calls happen — your contact list stays current without data entry.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Agent texting" icon="message">
    Confirmations, links, and follow-ups sent by the agent as part of the call.
  </Card>
