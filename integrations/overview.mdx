Owners and Admins manage integrations in **Settings → Integrations**. Integrations are on the **Growth** plan and above; on Starter the section shows an upgrade prompt. See [Plans and billing](/billing/plans).

### Facebook Lead Ads

Brings new leads from your Facebook and Instagram Instant Forms into Vocal as soon as they submit. Instagram lead ads come through the same Facebook Page, so one connection covers both.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your Page">
    Open **Facebook Lead Ads** and click **Connect Facebook Page**. Sign in to Facebook and pick the Page that runs your ads.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what each form does">
    Your Page's lead forms load automatically. For each form you want to collect, choose **Save lead, don't call**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each new lead is saved as a contact in Vocal with the lead's form answers. Before your team calls or texts these leads, add a consent checkbox to the Instant Form (in the form's privacy section) that covers phone calls and text messages.

### Telegram

Ask about your calls from Telegram: summaries, outcomes, and transcripts, answered by [Iris](/iris). Open **Telegram** and click **Connect to Telegram**, open the Vocal bot, and press **Start** in the chat. The page connects on its own when you come back. Owners and Admins can connect, and each person links their own Telegram account.
