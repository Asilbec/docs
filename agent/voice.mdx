The voice settings sit at the bottom of an agent's **Agent** page. **Selected voice** shows the current voice with a play button; click **Change voice** to pick another.

## Suggested voices

**Choose a voice** opens with six voices picked for business calls, each with a short description and a preview:

| Voice | Sounds |
| --- | --- |
| Ava, Sophie, Grace | Female, American |
| Marcus, Ethan | Male, American |
| Oliver | Male, British |

Select one and click **Save voice**. The new-agent wizard offers the same list on its **Basics** step.

## Browse all voices

For something else, click **Browse all voices** and search the full voice library. You can filter by **Gender** (**Any**, **Female**, **Male**) and **Accent** (**Any**, **US**, **UK**), and **Hide novelty voices** is on until you switch it off. The filters are best-effort, so play a voice before you save it.

Changing the voice doesn't touch the agent's [behavior](/agent/behavior); it says the same things in a different voice.

## Voice tuning

- **Speaking rate** runs from 0.70x to 1.20x. Slow it down a little if drivers keep asking the agent to repeat pay details.
- **Latency mode** trades speed for fidelity. **Balanced** (about 300 ms) replies faster and suits most live calls; **Normal** (about 500 ms) sounds slightly better when speed matters less.
- **Endpointing delay** is how long the agent waits after the caller stops talking before it answers, from 0.20 to 2 seconds (0.30 by default). If it keeps cutting in while someone reads out a load number or pauses to think, raise it a little.

## Hear it before callers do

Use **Preview** at the bottom of the editor's left column. It calls the agent in your browser using the draft, so it plays the voice you just picked. Calling the agent's phone number doesn't test a change: that call gets the published voice.

The voice, speaking rate and latency mode wait for **Publish**. The endpointing delay is stored with the agent's settings and applies to live calls as soon as you change it. [What Publish controls](/agent/behavior#what-publish-controls) has the full list.

<Tip>
  Pick for the people calling, not for yourself. A driver on a speakerphone in a truck cab needs a clear, steady voice more than a distinctive one.
</Tip>
