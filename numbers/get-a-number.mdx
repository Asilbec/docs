Owners and Admins manage numbers in **Settings → Numbers**. A new number rings the people on it and sends missed calls to voicemail.

## Add a number

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Numbers">
    Click **Add a new number**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search">
    Type an area code (412 for a Pittsburgh recruiting line, say), a toll-free prefix such as 800 or 888, or a few digits you'd like the number to contain. Each result shows whether it's local or toll-free and where it's from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Buy">
    If your plan still has an included number free, the number is added right away. Otherwise it's a paid number, which only the workspace Owner can add, through checkout.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Every member on a paid plan comes with one included number; extra numbers are priced on [Plans and billing](/billing/plans). Numbers also get added a few other ways:

- A teammate who joined without picking a number sees **Pick my number** on the home page and can claim the one their seat includes.
- Under **Teammates without a number** in **Settings → Numbers**, Owners and Admins can click **Assign a number** for that person.
- Group managers can click **Add number** on their group in **Settings → Groups**, and they see the price before confirming. See [Groups](/team/groups).

## What happens when someone calls

Every new number starts on its **Default call flow**. It rings everyone on the number's **Users** tab for 20 seconds, then sends the caller to voicemail. The person who added the number is its first user, and a number with nobody on it goes straight to voicemail.

To change who rings or when voicemail picks up, see [Call flows](/calls/call-flows).

## Texting

Local numbers can text US contacts once your business finishes [SMS registration](/numbers/sms-registration). Pick a local number for any line your recruiters or dispatchers text from. Texts show up in **Inbox** under **Chats**.

## Number settings

Click a number in the list to open it. You'll see up to three tabs:

- **General** has the number's name and emoji (so "Dallas recruiting" is easy to spot), a default ringtone for teammates who haven't picked their own, and **Carrier health**. Health reads Healthy, Degraded, Critical, or Unknown, based on how recent calls on the number went.
- **Users** lists who the number rings. Everyone here rings at once and the first to answer takes the call. They can also see the number's calls and texts. See [Ringing your team](/calls/ringing-your-team).
- **Call flow** shows the live flow and any others, with **Edit call flow**, **New flow**, and hold music.

## Release a number

At the bottom of the **General** tab, click **Release** and confirm. The number keeps working, and keeps billing, for 30 days. It shows **Grace period** in the list, and you can click **Keep this number** any time before then.

After 30 days the number is disconnected and goes back to the carrier, so you almost certainly won't get it back. Its call flow and users are cleared; past calls and texts stay. Numbers you transferred in can't be released here, so email [team@vocal.solutions](mailto:team@vocal.solutions) for those.
