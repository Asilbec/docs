A campaign is a list of people to call. Owners and Admins create and run campaigns from **Campaigns** in the sidebar; Members don't see it. The first question is **Who makes these calls?**

- **Your team** (the default): reps work a queue one call at a time.
- **AI agent**: an agent dials on its own and keeps going until the list is done.

## Team campaigns

Setup has three steps: **Campaign**, **Leads** and **Team**. Under **Add your master list**, bring in leads with **Upload file** (CSV, XLSX or XLS), **Paste leads** (two columns, name and phone), or **Choose Google Sheet**. **Who should get the first leads?** splits the list across recruiters, evenly or by the number you set for each; enter 0 to assign someone's share later. Reps then call their leads from **Queue**.

## AI agent campaigns

The steps are **Who calls**, **Goal**, **Recipients** and **Review**.

**Who calls.** Pick an agent that can call out (its **Routing** is **Outbound only** or **Inbound + outbound**). It dials from the number in its **Profile**, and a campaign won't place any calls for an agent without one. Assigning a number there also routes that number's incoming calls to the agent.

**Goal.** A short description of what the campaign is for. What the agent says on the call comes from its [behavior](/agent/behavior) and [steps](/agent/workflow), so set those up first. This step also shows that the campaign uses the agent's own outcomes.

**Recipients.** Upload a CSV, XLSX or XLS file with one row per person and tell Vocal which column holds the phone number. Every other column becomes a variable the agent can use: a column named "Load Number" is `{{contact.load_number}}`, so an outbound opener can say "Hi `{{contact.name}}`, calling about load `{{contact.load_number}}`." The step then tells you how many rows are ready to call, skipped, or unusable. Numbers on your workspace's do-not-call list, and numbers already in another active campaign, are skipped.

**Review.** Under **When should it start?** choose **Right away** or **At a set time**. **Calling hours and pacing** has:

- **Only call between set hours**, in your computer's time zone.
- **Calls at once**: how many calls run together. Leave it blank to use the agent's **Concurrency limit** (in its **Settings**), which starts at 1. Your plan also caps concurrent calls; see [Plans](/billing/plans).
- **Max calls per day**, blank for no cap.

## Running a campaign

The campaign page has **Start**, **Pause** and **Resume**. Resuming puts back in line any contacts that were stuck partway through a dial. **Add contacts** imports another file into the same campaign.

**No answer:** the contact is tried again 4 hours later, up to 3 attempts in total. You can't change these numbers today.

**Voicemail:** the agent follows **Settings** › **Voicemail** › **When an outbound call reaches voicemail**. **End the call** is the default and doesn't retry. **Call back later** tries again after the hours in **Call back after** (24 by default), and those callbacks count toward the same 3 attempts. **Leave a voicemail** leaves a message, with an optional **Voicemail script**. Detection is best-effort, since the agent recognizes a voicemail greeting by what it hears.

## Results

The campaign page shows **Total calls**, **Completed** and **Answer rate**, plus a count for each outcome, and each recipient's row shows its status and attempts. **Reports** opens the campaign's reporting. Answered calls also show up in [Inbox](/inbox), like any other call.

## Calling within the rules

This isn't legal advice, but a few US rules apply to almost every campaign:

- The FCC ruled in February 2024 that AI-generated voices count as "artificial" voices under the TCPA, so AI calls to cell phones need the person's prior consent, and marketing calls need it in writing.
- Telemarketing calls are limited to 8 a.m. to 9 p.m. in the recipient's local time. **Only call between set hours** helps, but it uses one time zone for the whole list.
- When someone asks not to be called again, stop calling them. In team campaigns, a rep marking someone do-not-call adds the number to the workspace list that future imports skip.
