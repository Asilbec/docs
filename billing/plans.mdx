Vocal pricing has two parts that you pick independently: a **team plan** (per member, the features your team uses) and an **AI usage plan** (how many calls your AI handles). No contracts, no sales calls required — manage everything in **Settings → Billing**.

## Team plans

Billed per member per month. Every active member also includes **one phone number**.

| | Starter | Growth | Scale | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Price (monthly) | $20/member | $35/member | $55/member | Custom |
| Price (annual) | $16/member | $28/member | $44/member | Custom |
| AI agents | 1 | 5 | Unlimited | Custom |
| Simultaneous calls | 2 | 10 | 25 | Custom |

## AI usage (credits)

AI usage is measured in the fairest unit we could find: **connected calls**. One credit covers one answered call with up to 3 minutes of AI talk time; each additional minute is one more credit.

What **doesn't** use credits:

- Calls nobody answered — dials, busy signals, rings that went nowhere
- Test calls while you're building your agent
- Calls your team answers themselves on the Vocal app
- The human part of a transferred call — the AI's minutes count, your team's don't

| Plan | Monthly price | Included calls | Extra calls |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 20 | — (pauses at 0) |
| Lite | $29 | 250 | $0.16/call |
| Pro | $79 | 800 | $0.14/call |
| Scale | $199 | 2,500 | $0.12/call |
| Volume | $499 | 6,000 | $0.11/call |

Prefer not to subscribe for usage? **Prepaid packs** never expire and roll over: $49 → 200 calls, $199 → 850, $499 → 2,200, $999 → 4,800. Included plan calls are always used first; prepaid covers the spillover.

## Phone numbers

- **Included:** one number per active member, on every paid team plan.
- **Additional numbers:** local $5/month, toll-free $8/month.
- Porting a number in is free — see [Port your existing number](/numbers/porting).

## One-time costs

- **US SMS registration — $99 one-time** when you enable business texting; it covers the carriers' registration fees and our compliance review. See [SMS registration](/numbers/sms-registration).

## The free trial

Every new workspace starts with a **14-day trial** including **50 connected AI calls** — enough to run Vocal on a real line, not just kick tires. You add a card up front but aren't charged until the trial ends; cancel before then and you pay nothing.

## Common billing questions

- **Can I change plans?** Anytime — upgrades take effect immediately, and billing is prorated by our payment provider.
- **Can I cancel?** Anytime, from **Settings → Billing**. Your service runs through the end of the period you've paid for.
- **What happens if I run out of included calls?** On Lite and up, extra calls bill at your plan's per-call rate. On Free, the AI pauses until you add a pack or upgrade — your team can still take calls directly.
