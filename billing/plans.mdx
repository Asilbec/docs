You pay for Vocal in two ways: a plan for each member of your team, and usage for texts. There's no contract. The Owner manages the plan in **Settings → Plan & billing**.

## Plans

Plans are priced per member per month. Annual billing costs less per month.

| | Starter | Growth | Scale |

| Monthly billing | \$30 / member | \$35 / member | \$45 / member |
| Annual billing | \$20 / member | \$25 / member | \$35 / member |
| Included numbers | 1 per member | 1 per member | 1 per member |

Every plan includes calling and texting to the US and Canada. Enterprise plans are priced for your team; [contact sales](https://www.vocal.solutions/contact-sales).

## Usage

| What | Price |
|---|---|
| Texts | 1¢ per SMS segment, sent or received |

**An SMS segment** is one text message's worth of characters. A long message splits into several segments, and each counts separately. Emoji and some special characters shrink a segment to 70 characters.

## Phone numbers

- Each member on a paid plan gets **one number included**.
- Extra numbers cost **\$5/month (local)** or **\$8/month (toll-free)**. The app shows the price before you buy.
- Numbers you [transfer from another carrier](/numbers/porting) use your included numbers first. Any beyond that cost the same monthly price, and we set up that billing with you before the transfer.

## Texting registration

US carriers require a one-time business registration before you can text. At registration you pay \$20.09 for the brand registration and campaign review, plus the first three months of your texting plan, **\$26.27 in total**. After that the texting plan costs \$2.06/month. Sole proprietors register under a different plan: \$50.99 at registration, then \$10.30/month. See [SMS registration](/numbers/sms-registration).

## Free trial

Your workspace's first subscription starts with **14 days free**. You add a card when you pick a plan, and it isn't charged until the trial ends; cancel before then and you pay nothing.
